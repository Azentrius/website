## 2. The website azentrius.com

### Server log files

When this website and our analytics service are accessed, our web server automatically records the following data in access logs:

- Truncated IP address of the requesting computer (the last IPv4 block is set to 0; for IPv6, only the first 32 bits are retained)
- Date and time of access
- Requested URL
- HTTP status code
- Amount of data transferred

These access logs are kept for no more than 14 days.

**Error and security logs:** To analyse errors and detect and prevent attacks, we also keep logs that may contain full IP addresses, timestamps and technical details of the request or security event. These logs are also kept for no more than 14 days. The separate access logs used by fail2ban for automatic attack detection are kept for no more than two days.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to keep the website and analytics service secure, resolve faults and prevent attacks.

### Audience measurement (Plausible Analytics)

To understand which content is used and how we can improve the website, we use **Plausible Analytics**. This open-source software is **self-hosted** by Azentrius on a server at Hetzner Online GmbH (analytics.azentrius.com). Visitor data is not sent to the maker of Plausible. Hetzner provides the server infrastructure; see “Hosting and data recipients” for details.

Plausible uses no cookies and does not place a persistent visitor identifier on your device. During a visit, the following is recorded:

- The page opened, the time of the event, clicks on links to other websites including the destination URL, and copying of commands
- The referring website (referrer)
- Browser, operating system and device type (derived from the user agent)
- Approximate location at the level of country, region and city (derived from the IP address)
- Scroll depth and active time spent on a page

Plausible processes the IP address and user agent to determine the approximate location and a daily visitor identifier. The full IP address and raw user agent are not stored in the analytics database. To create the identifier, Plausible forms a hash from the IP address, user agent, domain and a random value that changes daily. The random value is rotated and deleted every 24 hours. This prevents recognition across days using this identifier.

Plausible stores individual event and session records from which aggregated statistics are calculated. The daily change of the random value does not delete these records.

Event and session records are stored in the active analytics database for 24 months and then deleted. Database backups are kept for up to six months. Records already deleted from the active database may therefore remain in backups until that retention period expires.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to understand how our content is used and to improve the website using aggregated statistics. We explain your right to object in the separate “Right to object” section.

## 3. Contact by email

If you send us an email, we process your email address, your name if provided, the message content and any attachments. We use these details to handle your enquiry and reply to you.

Our email mailbox is hosted exclusively by Hetzner Online GmbH. Incoming messages are not forwarded to another email provider.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to answer questions about our projects and services. If your enquiry concerns a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

You decide which details to send us. Without a working reply address and the information needed for your enquiry, we may be unable to answer it.

We delete the correspondence once the enquiry has been fully resolved and the data is no longer needed for it. Where statutory retention obligations apply or the data is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we retain it for that purpose until the relevant reason no longer applies.
